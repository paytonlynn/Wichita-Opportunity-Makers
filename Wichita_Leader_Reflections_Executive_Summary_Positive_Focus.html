What leaders said

Selected quotes illustrate the most consistent themes in the open responses.

Assets already in place

“We have quality Tier 1 curriculum and supports for teachers in using them.”
“There is a foundation of high quality instructional materials, non-evaluative observation structures, and a process for aligning school action steps to district goals.”
“Our MTSS universal screening and diagnostics are widely used and understood by staff.”

Priority for improvement

“We must require teachers to use adopted HQIM.”
“Align resources across settings which will need protected time for staff to discuss.”
“Create the conditions for consistency and coherence by providing protected time for educators to have structured collaboration centered around access to grade level materials.”

Leadership support requested

“They need a clear definition of quality instruction so they know what the target is.”
“Permission to make needed changes and central office support to be the instructional leader.”
“How to provide high quality feedback. Know what they should be seeing and hearing in pd, plc and in the classroom and what to do if these things are not happening.”

Three leadership moves to prioritize

01 • DEFINE

Clarify the Vision

Name what quality grade-level instruction looks and sounds like across classrooms, PLCs, intervention, and professional learning.

02 • ALIGN

Connect intervention to Tier 1

Protect structured collaboration time so teams can align materials, student supports, and decisions across core and intervention settings.

03 • REINFORCE

Turn walkthroughs into focused support

Build leaders’ skill in recognizing the vision, giving actionable feedback, removing barriers, and following through on a narrow set of priorities.
